Site 1600

Site Safety Plan — the essentials

1600 Mount Fee Road, Whistler, BC

What is on this site

  • Building 1 — 5 storeys, 105 apartments.
  • Buildings 2, 3, 4 and 5 — townhouses, 5 units each. 20 townhomes.
  • One level of parkade under the buildings.

Muster point, first aid, extinguishers

Muster point: In front of the WDC site office.

First aid: WDC site trailer.

Fire extinguishers: Two per level in Building 1, one per townhome, and in the parkade beside the stairs and the main entrance.

Hazards and what we do

Falling from height
Guardrails stay up. If you have to take one down, you tie off first and you put it back. From 10 ft up you need fall protection. A written plan only if you could fall 25 ft or more and there are no permanent guardrails.
Something falling on you
Tape off the area below overhead work and put up a sign. Look down before you start. Use tool lanyards. Never leave windows, doors or drywall leaning — strap them or lay them flat.
Fire
No hot work without a Hot Work Permit. Clear or cover the combustibles. Keep a hose or extinguisher right there. Fire watch during the work and 60 minutes after. Before any break: propane off, torch on its stand, hand the fire watch to a person — never just walk away.
Crane and mobile equipment
Certified operators only. Check the machine before the shift. Make eye contact with the operator before you get close. No load passes over anyone. Where two cranes or pumps share a zone, the daily Overlapping Zone form gets signed.
Dust and silica
Score and snap drywall by hand. Power tools only with HEPA extraction. Never dry sweep and never use compressed air. Wet clean at the end of the shift. P100 respirator and safety glasses.
Chemicals
Every container needs its label. The SDS is on the QR code at the Safety Board and the Lock Room. Do not use a product you have not been trained on.
Electrical
GFCI on all temporary power. Cords out of the water and out of the walkway. Keep panels clear. Lock out before working on anything live.
Trips and mess
Stairs, hallways, exits and extinguishers stay clear. Clean as you go. Delivery rooms are drop-off only — move the material to the suite the same day, two hours maximum.
Wildlife and weather
Food waste goes in the food waste bin in the Crew Parkade (Lot 6). Everything else you take with you. The sprinklers by the forest run morning and end of day. Clear snow and ice from the walkways.
