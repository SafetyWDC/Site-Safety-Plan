Site Safety Plan — the essentials

1825 Mount Fee Road (Lot 6), Whistler, BC

What is on this site

  • Lot 6, Cheakamus Phase 2. Steep rocky ground being cleared, blasted and serviced for a future residential development.
  • The lot is built in three phases. This plan covers all three — the work and the hazards are the same in each one.
  • Phase 1 — under construction. Strata Road A and Strata Road B, and the services that go under them.
  • Phase 2 — the south and east parts of the lot.
  • Phase 3 — the north part of the lot.
  • The phase boundaries are on the Webster Engineering overall plan (EGBC Permit No. 1001444).

Muster point, first aid, extinguishers

Muster point: Shared with Site 1600 — in front of the WDC site office.

First aid: Shared with Site 1600 — the WDC site trailer.

Fire extinguishers: On every machine and at the fuel and equipment area.

Hazards and what we do

Blasting
Certified blaster only, working to the approved blast design. Mats down, site cleared and guarded, warning signals before and after. Nobody goes back in until the blaster gives the all-clear. If a charge does not go off, stay away and let the blaster handle it.
Digging and loose ground
Anything over 4 ft deep needs to be sloped, benched or shored. Keep the dirt pile and the machines back from the edge. Scale the loose rock after every blast. Ladder within 8 m of anyone in a trench. Stop work on the slope during heavy rain.
Mobile equipment
Certified operators only. Check the machine before the shift and write it in the log. Seat belt on. Use of spotter when you cannot see. No foot traffic on haul roads.
Traffic and the neighbours
Certified traffic control at the roundabout. Stick to the truck route and the speed limit. Clean the mud and rock off the public road the same day. Notify the neighbours before a blast or a road closure.
Buried and overhead services
Get the locates and mark them before you dig. Hand-dig within 1 m of a marked line. Stay back from the overhead lines and use a spotter.
Dust and noise
Water or dust collection on the drill. Wear hearing protection during the drilling and blasting. Respirator per the silica plan. Keep the haul road wet.
Falling trees
Certified fallers only. Nobody else within two tree lengths. Plan your escape route before you cut.
Working alone
Set a check-in time and a person to check in with. Carry a radio. Tell the CSO or the Superintendent before anyone works alone.
Fire
No hot work without a permit. Extinguisher and water on hand. Fire watch during and 60 minutes after. No hot work when fire danger is high.
Wildlife and weather
Food waste goes in the food waste bin in the Crew Parkade (Lot 6). Everything else you take with you. Watch for heat and cold.
